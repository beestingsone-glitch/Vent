Child Safety Standards & CSAE Policy

App: Vent

Developer: Lucas Auto Code LAC LLC

Zero Tolerance for CSAE and CSAM

Vent maintains a zero-tolerance policy regarding Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE). Any attempt to produce, share, transmit, or solicit illicit content involving minors is strictly prohibited.

Reporting and Enforcement

Users can report abusive conversations, solicitations, or safety concerns directly within the app using the in-app reporting and user-blocking mechanisms. When illegal material or child safety violations are detected or reported, accounts are permanently banned, associated data is purged, and reports are filed with regional authorities including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC).
